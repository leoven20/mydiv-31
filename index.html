History

Tal was borned in Riga, Latvia. As a child, he joined Riga Palace of Young Peoneers chess club. In 1949, he played Ratmir Kholmov, a young master who had recently competed in the prestigious Chigorin Memorial in 1947, in a simultaneous exhibition. Tal used an imaginative combination to win his game at the age of 13. Alexander Koblents began tutoring him in 1949, after which Tal's game rapidly improved.

Explanation

While Koblents developed his pieces, Tal just pressured on the right side of the board with the pawns. And after the relentless push of the G pawn it opens a row to Tal's rook.
